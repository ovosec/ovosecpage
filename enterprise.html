Enterprise Architecture

Built for Organization-Wide Control & Security

Designed from the ground up to separate corporate network operations from consumer traffic.

1. Flexible Seat & Resource Pool Allocation

Instead of rigid single-account subscriptions, your organization receives a unified Maximum Seat Pool, Total Device Pool, and Multi-TB Traffic Pool. Enterprise administrators can dynamically slice and assign custom device limits (e.g., 2 devices for staff, 10 devices for QA engineers) and bandwidth quotas per employee.

2. Dedicated Node Groups & Clean Static IPs

Deploy private, access-controlled server groups (such as dedicated Singapore SG, Tokyo, US West, or Frankfurt clusters) invisible to public subscribers. Ensure consistent, high-reputation static egress IPs for cloud consoles, GitHub/AWS whitelisting, cross-border e-commerce, and enterprise AI APIs.

3. Centralized Enterprise Seat Console

Enterprise administrators log directly into the dedicated Enterprise Seat Management Console on the web portal. Provision new employee accounts in seconds, monitor real-time traffic & active devices across the team, revoke access immediately upon offboarding, and manage a single unified renewal date.

4. Pure, Distraction-Free Enterprise Client

When employees sign in to OVOSEC on Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, or Linux, the client automatically switches to ENTERPRISE Mode—hiding all personal VIP/PRO upsells, free-time lotteries, and referral banners, while unlocking both manual dedicated node selection and intelligent auto-connect.
